# P0 — Publication and historical-grade boundary

Depends on 20271204031000 (PR #496). Do not deploy the application before both migrations are verified. Preflight on production found 557 historical snapshots with classifications and reproduced cross-student historical access; 224 unpublished grades in two paid enrollments. No production records were changed.

The database owns the publication predicate: exact enrollment session + class, SUCCESS, nonblank PDF path, quarterly type and valid number. Annual results require a distinct annual SUCCESS sheet in the same session. An annual sheet does not publish individual quarters. Imported history without a valid enrollment/session, closed snapshot, and annual sheet remains institutional-only; official document issuance retains its separate contract. Payment grants never carry across enrollments. Staff retains read access, teachers retain assigned subject scope, progression remains operational regardless of bulletin entitlement. Reappraisal filing/decision ACLs are unchanged; the read boundary validates enrollment, student, session and class.

The old synthetic suite runs first, followed by schema extension and the complementary migration. CI is not a full-schema replay. Before applying both migrations, verify production-grade publication semantics (including annual sheet validity, document issuance and historical legacy records), full legal engine, remaining privileged RPCs, and signed storage URLs. Apply both migrations consecutively in a controlled release; do not publish app code between them. No fiscal changes, payment edits, or history rewrites.
